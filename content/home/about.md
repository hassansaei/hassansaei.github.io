\ 

I am a human geneticist at the Laboratory of Hereditary Renal Diseases, Imagine Institute (Paris), who combines genomics with organoid-based functional modeling to decode non-coding variants in hereditary kidney disease, with the goal of better diagnosis and new gene therapies. My work sits in translational computational genomics: I use perturbation approaches, both in silico and experimental, to connect deep intronic and regulatory variation to its functional consequences and to turn that knowledge into improved diagnostics and therapy development.
