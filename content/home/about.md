\ 

I am a postdoctoral researcher in RNA biology and gene therapy at the Laboratory of Hereditary Renal Diseases, Imagine Institute (Paris). My research combines genomics with organoid-based functional modeling to decode non-coding variants in hereditary kidney disease. Working at the interface of translational computational genomics and gene therapy, I use both in silico and experimental perturbation approaches to link deep intronic and regulatory variants to their functional consequences, and to translate these insights into improved diagnostics and new therapeutic strategies.
